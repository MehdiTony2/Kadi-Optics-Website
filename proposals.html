Strategic Note

Positioning shift, July 2026
Following program-officer guidance on the ART Phase II proposal, KADI's near-term proposals (ART, and future applications other than the NSF SBIR resubmission) now lead with the already-validated intraoperative/surgical probe as the near-term commercial focus, with ECMO/ICU and interventional cath lab as the next expansion platforms. The noninvasive/transcutaneous wearable remains the long-term destination but is now framed as a future direction rather than the near-term ask, since it is earlier-stage and was flagged by reviewers as the central open technical risk in the original NSF submission.
