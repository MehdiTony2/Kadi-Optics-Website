Peer-Reviewed Publications (Clinical Coagulation Series)

Chronological — the direct scientific basis for KADI Optics' clinical claims, all from cardiopulmonary bypass (CPB) studies at Arnold Palmer Hospital for Children, Orlando Health.

YearTitleVenueSignificance
2016 Optical Rheology of Blood during Cardiovascular Surgery CLEO 2016 (conf.) First public demonstration of the fiber-optic LC-DLS instrument integrated into CPB equipment.
2017 Real-time intraoperative monitoring of blood coagulability via coherence-gated light scattering Nature Biomedical Engineering 1, 0028 Flagship paper. Full theoretical framework + first clinical correlation study (N=10). Differential correlation vs. ACT: r=−0.76, p=0.0001.
2018 Intraoperative Assessment of Blood Coagulability using Coherence-gated Light Scattering CLEO/Biophotonics Congress 2018 Conference restatement of the 2017 paper's theory and results.
2020 Passive Coagulability Assay Based on Coherence-Gated Light Scattering Hemato 1(2), 49–59 Scaled clinical validation to N=30. Formalized single figure-of-merit γ. Differential ACT correlation r=−0.73, p<0.0001; TEG Coagulation Index r=0.446.
2022 Passive high-frequency microrheology of blood Soft Matter 18, 2452–2461 Most mechanistically mature paper. Introduces a 3-parameter Maxwell viscoelastic model, validated against controlled thrombin/fibrinogen chemistry, applied to N=23 cohort.
